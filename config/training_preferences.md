# Preferred Split

5-day lifting split on a 7-day cycle:

- Tuesday: Chest + Back + Shoulders
- Wednesday: Arms
- Thursday: Legs
- Friday: Rest
- Saturday: Upper
- Sunday: Lower
- Monday: Rest

---

# Weekday vs. Weekend Training Philosophy

**Tuesday–Thursday (weekday sessions):**

- Less time and energy available.
- Focus on controlled movements, good form, and quality repetitions.
- Do not chase maximum weight or volume on these days.

**Saturday–Sunday (weekend sessions):**

- More time and better recovery available.
- Push intensity, lift heavier, and add a bit more volume.

---

# Daily Workout Structure

## Tuesday — Chest + Back + Shoulders

Core workout:

- Lat pulldown
- Incline chest press (dumbbell, barbell, or machine)
- Mid-row (e.g. T-bar row)
- Flat bench press (dumbbell, barbell, or machine)
- Shoulder press
- Dumbbell shrugs (traps)

Optional finisher if energy allows: an additional pulling movement, such as assisted pull-ups.

## Wednesday — Arms

- Bicep curls
- Tricep pushdowns
- Preacher curls
- Second tricep exercise: rotate between **single-arm cross-body cable extension** and **cable kickbacks** (picked day-of). Both avoid skull crushers and overhead tricep extensions, which bother the elbows.
- Cable lateral raises (**IMPORTANT** — always include)
- Dumbbell wrist curls (forearms — 1 of 2 weekly sessions)

## Thursday — Legs

Machine-focused. Barbell squats bother the shoulders and RDLs aren't yet comfortable; machines also allow training harder.

- Seated or 45-degree decline leg press
- Seated leg extensions
- Seated hamstring curls
- Calf raises
- Optional: rear delt flies (quick to add, doesn't interfere much with the rest of the session)

## Saturday — Upper

Built around favorite compound movements:

- Incline chest press
- One back movement (lat pulldown or mid-row, depending on how the day feels)
- Preacher curls
- A tricep exercise
- Lat raises
- Dumbbell wrist curls (forearms — 2 of 2 weekly sessions)

More room for variety here — rotate in:

- Chest fly (machine or cable)
- Cable chest press
- Alternative back exercises
- Incline dumbbell curls
- Other accessory movements

Still keep it built around exercises that are genuinely enjoyed — that's what sustains consistency.

## Sunday — Lower

For now, keep this identical to Thursday's leg routine. Having a consistent leg routine at all is already a big improvement (none since ~2022). Prioritize getting progressively stronger within this routine over introducing variation.

## Monday & Friday — Rest

Full rest from lifting.

# Exercises to Avoid

- Skull crushers (elbow discomfort)
- Overhead tricep extensions (elbow discomfort)
- Barbell squats (shoulder discomfort)
- RDLs (not yet comfortable with the movement — revisit later, not a permanent exclusion)

# Preferred Workout Length

- Lifting only: ~60–90 minutes.
- Lifting + cardio in the same session: ~90–120 minutes total.

---

# Cardio

3 days per week: **Monday, Wednesday, Friday**.

Session: 30–40 minutes of one of:

- Incline walking
- Slight incline running
- Stair machine

Intensity: for running and stairs, target speed ~4–5. Higher speeds aren't sustainable for a full session (burnout within ~10 minutes). Moderate, sustainable intensity beats pushing too hard and skipping sessions.

Guiding principle: **low expectations, high consistency.**

Monday and Friday cardio can stay light since they're already lifting rest days. Wednesday cardio pairs with the Arms lifting session.

Time horizon / checkpoint: **December** — a natural point to evaluate progress and decide whether changes are needed.

---

# Progression

Prioritize progressive overload.

Prefer adding reps before adding weight when appropriate.
